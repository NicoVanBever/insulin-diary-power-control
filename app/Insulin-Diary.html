Lab results & check-ups

Enter the plan your doctor or diabetes nurse gave you This app does not decide how much insulin you need. It records your prescribed plan, reminds you, and warns you about possible mistakes (like taking the same insulin twice). Change these numbers only when your care team tells you to.

1 · My insulins

"Min. hours between doses" powers the double-dose warning. For once-daily basal insulin, 10–20 h is typical; set it to match your plan.

2 · Daily schedule

3 · Correction scale (only if your doctor gave you one)

Copy it exactly. When you log a rapid/mealtime dose shortly after a reading, the app will show you what your scale says for that reading. It does not add it for you.

4 · Mealtime ratios (Type 1 / carb counting)

Write these exactly as your diabetes team gave them. They are shown as a reminder when you log a rapid-acting dose and printed on your report. The app does not calculate doses from them.

5 · Supplies (pens, cartridges, vials)

The app counts down the units in your current pen or vial as you log doses, including the small safety shot before each injection, and warns you before it runs out or passes its use-by period. Check your insulin's leaflet for how long it keeps once opened.

How syncing works

  1. Phone and PC on the same network: the same Wi-Fi or router (the PC may use a cable), the PC's mobile hotspot, the phone's hotspot, or the phone connected to the PC by USB cable with USB tethering switched on.
  2. No app on the phone yet? Point the phone's camera at the code. A page from this PC opens where you can download and install the app.
  3. App installed? In the app: Sync → Scan code on PC. Each side sends what the other is missing. If this PC has a newer version of the phone app, the phone offers to update.

Every code is unique and carries its own 256-bit key, made from this PC's private key. Everything sent between phone and PC is encrypted with it (AES-256-GCM); the key itself never travels over the network. A code works for one sync and changes every 5 minutes. No account, no cloud. The first time, Windows may ask whether to allow Insulin Diary on your network: choose Private networks and click Allow.

No shared network?

Move your diary with a file instead. Save a diary file here, copy it to the other device (USB cable, e-mail to yourself or a cloud drive) and open it there with Merge a diary file. Then do the same the other way round. Entries from both sides are kept.

Install the phone app without a network

The phone app is built into this program, so no internet or Wi-Fi is needed.

  1. Click Show the phone app file.
  2. Connect the phone with a USB cable and choose File transfer on the phone.
  3. Copy the file to the phone's Download folder.
  4. On the phone, open the file (Files → Downloads) and tap Install. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.

No cable? Turn on this PC's mobile hotspot, connect the phone to it and scan the code with the phone's camera.

  1. On your PC, open Insulin Diary → Sync with phone.
  2. Keep this phone on the same network as the PC: the same Wi-Fi, a hotspot from one of them, or a USB cable with USB tethering on.
  3. Scan the code. New entries go both ways.
